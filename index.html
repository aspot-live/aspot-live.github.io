This is not therapy. Inner Dashboard is a general self-reflection guide built on common psychological ideas. It is not a diagnosis or treatment and can’t replace a trained therapist. For heavy trauma, very strong feelings, or if you feel unsafe, please work with a trained professional or contact your local emergency services or a crisis line.

Looking for support? BetterHelp ↗ is an online platform for connecting with a therapist. It is one option among many.

References and sources
  • Attachment Project ↗: attachment style test and information.
  • Bartholomew & Horowitz (1991), “Attachment styles among young adults: a test of a four-category model”, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology: the four attachment styles used here.
  • 16Personalities ↗: personality types and nicknames such as Architect.
  • IFS Institute ↗: Internal Family Systems, developed by Richard Schwartz.
  • Beck Institute ↗: cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), including thinking traps.
  • ITAA ↗: Transactional Analysis and the Parent-Adult-Child model, developed by Eric Berne.
  • Joines & Stewart (2002), Personality Adaptations: A New Guide to Human Understanding in Psychotherapy and Counseling: the six personality adaptations from Transactional Analysis.
  • The 5-4-3-2-1 exercise: a widely used grounding technique.
  • Calm: The Feelings Wheel ↗: source of the feelings wheel used here.

Links between personality, attachment, core fear and feelings are common associations, not exact rules. Personality type labels are popular tools, not clinical measures.
